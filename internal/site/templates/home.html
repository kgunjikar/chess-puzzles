How the puzzles are made

Each position comes from a computer game where one side made a mistake. Stockfish searches every candidate deeply and keeps it only if:

  • at every step, exactly one attacking move mates fastest (no "duals"),
  • the defender always plays the longest resistance,
  • the line really ends in checkmate.

While you solve, a copy of Stockfish runs in your browser. If you find a different move that still forces mate in time, it counts.
