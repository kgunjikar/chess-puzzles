About {{.Site.Title}}

{{.Site.Title}} is a free collection of checkmate puzzles, from mate in 1 to mate in 5. The puzzles come from computer-played games. Each one is kept only if Stockfish confirms a single forced solution.

Open source and licenses

The site's code is open source{{if .Site.SourceURL}} at {{.Site.SourceURL}}{{end}}.

When you solve a puzzle, this site sends your browser a copy of Stockfish 19 built for WebAssembly by stockfish.js. Stockfish is free software under the GNU General Public License v3. Its full source code is at github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish, and the WebAssembly build's source is at github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js. This site uses the engine unmodified.

The puzzle positions and solutions on this site may be reused freely.
